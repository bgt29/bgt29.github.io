About this site

This site was built from scratch — no website builders or ready-made templates. Here’s what it can do, how to use it and where the easter eggs are hidden.

Features

Make it yoursFive brightness levels — from Night to Daylight, your own accent color and the scale of the whole site. It’s all in the gear menu, and your browser remembers it.
Living backgroundSoft spots in the background slowly flow like terrain on a map and follow the color you pick.
Russian and EnglishVisitors from other countries get the English version straight away. You can switch the language in the gear menu.
Fits any screenOn a phone, swipe between tabs; pages change smoothly without reloading. On a large monitor the column gets wider and small text larger; on a small phone the text fits the width.
Project galleriesPhotos loop around — use the arrows, the keys, a finger or the mouse: you can drag the strip. Portrait shots get a soft glow in the photo’s own colors.
Short linkskirillbuldakov.ru/tg leads to Telegram, plus /vk, /youtube, /avito and more — easy to say out loud or print.
QR code and one-tap copyA button on the profile card shows the site’s QR code — point a camera and open it. And a button next to the links copies the username or the address.
A terminal of its ownA real command line: commands in Russian and English, mini games, coffee and tea and a few surprises. Where to find it — see the easter eggs below.
ArticlesPractical tips on building and servicing PCs, based on posts from my Telegram channel, with links to the original posts (in Russian). The latest ones are in a strip on the home page.
Message in one tapThe Telegram button opens a chat with a message already started for the service you need. And there’s always a VK button next to it — not everyone has Telegram, or can use it.
Build cost calculatorThe PC build page has a calculator: enter a budget or move the slider and see the price of the work. The Telegram button sends the estimate as a message; VK is right next to it.
Found a bug?The footer has links to Telegram and VK. Telegram opens with a ready-made message and the page address.

Controls

  • Gear, top rightbrightness, accent color, scale and language; resetting the appearance is there too
  • Swipe left or righton a phone — the next tab
  • Drag with the mouseproject rows in the portfolio, the article strip on the home page and gallery photos — just like a finger on a phone
  • ← →browse photos in a project gallery — they loop around
  • Terminal: drag the top barmove the window; the bottom-right corner resizes it
  • − and +, Ctrl + wheelin the terminal — text size
  • ↑ ↓in the terminal — previous commands
  • Double-click the barput the terminal window back in place and size
  • Games: arrows or WASD, Spacemove, rotate, jump; on a phone — the buttons under the field, taps and swipes
  • Escin a game — quit the game, otherwise — close the terminal

Easter eggs

Found them yourself? Great. If not — hints below; the answers are hidden, tap to reveal.

The terminalSomewhere on the home page a yellow cursor is blinking…

Tap the blinking cursor in the EBK KVN sign under Projects — a terminal opens. Type help to see what it can do. It understands Russian too, and even the wrong keyboard layout.

RGB modeLike the lighting in a gaming PC case.

Tap the gear three times quickly — the tile borders shimmer like a rainbow. Three more turn it off. Or type rgb in the terminal.

Baguette showerBaget isn’t just a nickname.

Tap the “Baget” nickname badge five times in a row — baguettes will fall from the sky. Or type baguette in the terminal.

A breakThe terminal knows more than help shows.

Type coffee, tea or hookah in the terminal — and see what happens.

Mini gamesThere’s something to play in the terminal.

Type games: Baguette Run — jump over bugs, Build It — stack PC parts into rows, Cable — collect hardware and dodge the bugs. Records are saved in your browser.

Secret commandsWhat if you try sudo?

sudo, rm -rf, hello — the terminal has an answer for each.

How it’s built

From scratchHTML, CSS and JavaScript — no frameworks, builders or ready-made themes.
Fast and lightA static site with no server or database. The content is built into the pages — it loads fast and reads fine even without JavaScript. Photos use the lightweight WebP format, the mini games load only when you start them, the next gallery photos load ahead of time, and animations run only where you can see them.
A protected domainHTTPS only, signed DNS (DNSSEC) and protection against forged emails in the site’s name (SPF and DMARC).
Search- and AI-friendlyStructured data for search engines, a sitemap, a summary for AI assistants (llms.txt) and a custom link preview for messengers.
Calm modeIf animations are turned off in your phone or computer settings, the site stays calm — no flying or shaking.
PrivacyVisit statistics only. Details are on the Privacy and cookies page.

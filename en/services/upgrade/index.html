What I work with

  • Honeywell PTM7950The thermal paste I use most: phase-change, conducts heat almost like liquid metal, doesn’t get squeezed out and lasts for years.
  • Fehonda and Laird Tputty 607 padsMy usual choice — the best value for money: regular pads for memory and power stages, liquid ones for gaps up to 1 mm.
  • Cleaning and lubricationPetroleum spirit removes old paste and degreases, Flux-Off cleans sockets, PMS-200 oil lubricates fan bearings.
  • My own test kitUSB drives with Windows and BIOS firmware, an SSD with test software, separate power buttons — faster, more reliable testing.

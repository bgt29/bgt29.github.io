Servicing an RTX 3070 EVGA FTW3

The client’s complaint: in games the card hit 90 °C, with black screens and heavy stutters. Inside, part of the thermal paste had been squeezed out, the VRM pad was the wrong size and didn’t press everywhere, and the other pads had dried out.

I replaced them with Honeywell PTM7950 and Fehonda pads and, as agreed with the client, added pads on the metal backplate side for the VRM and memory. Temperatures dropped noticeably.

  • Graphics cardRTX 3070 EVGA FTW3
  • Honeywell PTM7950 paste
  • Fehonda thermal pads
  • Backplate pads

A budget gaming PC: the basic minimum

A new computer for gaming and work for 57,000 ₽ including assembly: which parts went into it. Prices as of October 2025.

A new budget computer — the basic minimum in 2025 for gaming or work. Together with my work, it came to 57,000 ₽ (prices as of October 2025).

The parts

  • CPU — Intel Core i3-12100F
  • Motherboard — Maxsun Challenger H610M
  • RAM — 2 × 16 GB Netac Shadow S, 3200 MHz, CL16
  • Graphics card — ASRock Radeon RX 6600 Challenger
  • Power supply — GamerStorm (DeepCool) PF600X
  • CPU cooler — PentaWave Z04E V2
  • SSD — 1 TB Digma Meta M6
  • Case — Xastra A303M
  • ARGB and fan controller

All the parts were bought in Russia, with fast delivery and a warranty.

Cables

I like to route cables neatly and nicely, even though it takes more time — it gives me aesthetic pleasure.

Good to know

Parts have gone up in price since then, so the same computer would cost more now. And the case in this build arrived with a small factory defect — I warned the customer about it.

When it’s time to service your graphics card

How to tell that your graphics card needs new thermal paste and pads, and what it gives you — with examples from cards I’ve serviced.

Thermal paste and pads in a graphics card dry out and lose their properties over time, and the paste can simply get pumped out. The card starts running hotter and louder. Here are the signs — with examples from cards I’ve serviced.

The signs

  • High temperatures in games. A customer’s RTX 3070 EVGA FTW3 reached 90 °C in games.
  • A Hot Spot much higher than the GPU temperature. An HP RTX 3060 Ti showed 84 °C on the GPU and 100 °C on the Hot Spot in a stress test.
  • Black screens and heavy stutters — that’s what happened with the same RTX 3070 EVGA.
  • Loud fans: they have to spin faster to keep the temperature down.
  • The card hasn’t been serviced for a long time. On the RTX 3060 Ti, the thermal paste had simply been pumped out since the day it was bought.

What I do

I take the card apart, remove the old paste and pads, and clean the board and the heatsink. I apply Honeywell PTM7950 phase-change paste and Fehonda thermal pads, and Laird Tputty 607 liquid pads on some components. If the metal backplate has no pads where this model can have them, I add them after agreeing with the customer. If needed, I replace the fan bearing.

What it gives you

Numbers from my servicing jobs — GPU / Hot Spot:

  • RTX 3060 Ti HP: 84 / 100 °C before, 75 / 87 °C after, at the same 65% fan speed.
  • RTX 3080 EVGA FTW3 Ultra: 78 / 92 °C, memory 88 °C, 2,300 rpm before; 73 / 83 °C, memory 84 °C, 2,100 rpm after.
  • RTX 3080 Ti Inno3D iChill X4: 70 / 78 °C, memory 73 °C, about 1,700 rpm before; 67 / 74 °C, memory 70 °C, about 1,600 rpm after.

Phase-change paste reaches its full performance after a few heat-up and cool-down cycles — after a couple of days the temperatures drop even lower.

Why it matters who does the servicing

Once I got an RTX 3070 that had been serviced at a repair shop for 3,000 ₽. Inside, all the pads were dry and nothing had been cleaned: the shop had only replaced the thermal paste — and applied far too much of it.
